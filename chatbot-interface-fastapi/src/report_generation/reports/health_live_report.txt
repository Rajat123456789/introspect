# Consolidated Report on Health Data Visualizations

This report summarizes key findings from several health data visualizations, focusing on metrics such as steps, heart rate, activity types, weight trends, calorie intake/expenditure, sleep patterns, and more. Findings are organized by categories to present a comprehensive view.

## Activity Data

### Steps
- **Daily Step Count**: Step count fluctuates between 0 and approximately 12,000 steps per day, with peaks around March 1 and near-zero counts in mid-March.
- **Total Steps by Day of Week**: Highest on Thursday (~50,000), followed by Saturday (~45,000) and Sunday (~42,000), indicating more activity towards the end of the week.

### Activity Types
- **Exercise Session Types**: Type `79` is most frequent at 32.9% (79.0 sessions), while type `8` is least frequent at 2.9%.
- **Average Exercise Duration**: Type `8` sessions last the longest (~40 minutes), contrasting with its low frequency.
  
### Activity Patterns
- **Hourly Activity**: Peaks in activity are observed around early mornings and late afternoons, specifically 0, 3, and 17 hours with durations of 32-35 minutes.

## Heart Rate Data

### Resting Heart Rate
- **Trend**: Begins at over 110 BPM (Feb 15) but stabilizes between 50-80 BPM.
- **Average by Hour**: Highest early in the morning (~95 BPM at 3 AM), decreases to around 60 BPM in the afternoon then rises again.

### Distribution and Variability
- **Heart Rate Zones by Exercise Type**: Varies between 60-160 BPM across exercises.
- **Hourly Variation**: Median heart rate settles between 60-80 BPM, with fewer occurrences above 120 BPM.

## Weight and Body Composition

### Weight Trends
- **Weight**: Decreases from approximately 105 kg to 98 kg over February to April.
- **Body Fat**: Declines from 34% to 26%, especially notable around March 1.

### Distribution
- **BMI**: Gradually decreases from 34 to 31, correlating with weight loss.

## Nutritional Data

### Calories

## Sleep Patterns

### Consistency

## Summary of Correlations

### Key Correlations
- **Nutritional Intake**: Strong correlation between calorie intake and macronutrient consumption, particularly calories and fat (0.84).
- **Exercise and Heart Rate**: Variability across exercise types influencing heart rate zones significantly; different exercises impact resting heart rate trends.
